DISTRIBUTOR AFFILIATE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between:

FormYaar ("Company"), represented by Hemant Chauhan,
and
[Distributor Full Name], YouTube channel "[Channel Name]" ("Distributor")

Effective Date: [DATE]
Term: This is a fixed-term pilot agreement lasting 15 days from the
Effective Date. It does not auto-renew. It may end earlier only if
both parties agree in writing, or per Section 8.2 (either party's
right to end early by written notice).

1. PURPOSE
1.1 Distributor agrees to promote FormYaar's PAN card application
    service to their audience using a unique Coupon Code issued by the
    Company. In exchange, Company will pay Distributor a commission
    for each Tracked Referral, as defined below.
1.2 Minimum Deliverable. Within the 15-day term, Distributor agrees to
    publish at least one (1) video featuring FormYaar and the Coupon
    Code, on their YouTube channel, within the first 7 days of the
    Effective Date. Distributor may publish additional content
    referencing FormYaar during the term at their discretion, but only
    one video is required. If Distributor does not publish the
    required video within 7 days, Company may choose not to continue
    this Agreement for its remaining term, without further obligation
    beyond commission already earned to that point.
2. COMMISSION
2.1 Company will pay Distributor ₹14 (Rupees Fourteen) per Tracked Referral,
    unless a different rate is agreed in writing for a future period.
    Rate changes never apply retroactively.
2.2 Payment schedule for this Agreement: a single payout at the end of
    the 15-day term, covering all Tracked Referrals recorded during
    the term. No minimum payout threshold applies.
2.3 Payment via [UPI/bank transfer].

3. ATTRIBUTION & PAYOUT DATA
3.1 Definitions. The "Coupon Code" is the unique code FormYaar issues
    to the Distributor. A "Tracked Referral" is a completed, non-refunded
    customer payment on which the Distributor's Coupon Code was
    successfully applied at checkout and recorded by FormYaar's
    systems.
3.2 Sole source of truth. FormYaar's transaction and attribution
    records are the sole and conclusive basis for determining Tracked
    Referrals and calculating amounts payable. Attribution is
    determined exclusively by whether a customer applied the Distributor's
    Coupon Code at the time of payment, as recorded automatically by
    FormYaar's payment and webhook systems. No other measure — video
    views, clicks, impressions, comments, self-reported numbers, or
    third-party analytics — shall be used to determine or adjust
    attribution or payout.
3.3 Nature of code-based attribution. The Distributor acknowledges that a
    customer can be attributed to the Distributor only if that customer
    applies the Distributor's Coupon Code during payment. Customers who
    view the Distributor's content but do not apply the Coupon Code are
    not attributable and will not be counted. The Distributor agrees this
    is an inherent and accepted characteristic of coupon-based
    attribution.
3.4 Transparency & export. FormYaar provides the Distributor continuous,
    real-time visibility of their Tracked Referrals and earnings via
    the Distributor dashboard, and, on reasonable written request (up to
    once per calendar month), a data export of the Distributor's Tracked
    Referrals for a stated period.
3.5 Disputes. The Distributor may raise a dispute only as to whether a
    specific transaction was correctly recorded within FormYaar's
    system (e.g., a Coupon Code was applied but, due to a technical
    error, not counted), in writing within 14 days of the payout date
    on which that transaction should have appeared, citing the
    specific order or payment reference. The Distributor waives any right
    to challenge the attribution methodology itself, the choice of
    coupon-code tracking, or FormYaar's records as against any
    external or self-reported estimate of traffic, reach, or
    conversions. FormYaar will investigate bona-fide recording errors
    in good faith; FormYaar's determination after investigation is
    final.

4. CODE INTEGRITY & EXPIRY
4.1 The Coupon Code remains active for the full duration of this
    Agreement. The Distributor may distribute the Coupon Code as
    widely as they choose to maximize their own earnings.
4.2 Upon termination or expiry of this Agreement for any reason
    (either party's choice, per Section 8), the Coupon Code is
    deactivated immediately. Any subsequent attempted use of the
    Coupon Code is simply treated as an expired/invalid code at
    checkout — no accusation or investigation is implied.
4.3 Commission already earned on Tracked Referrals recorded prior to
    deactivation remains payable in full per the normal payment
    schedule in Section 2.2, regardless of how or why the Agreement
    ended.

5. CONTENT & IP
5.1 Distributor retains ownership of all content they produce.
5.2 Company may reference, clip, or repost content Distributor has already
    published (e.g., existing FormYaar-related videos) as case-study or
    marketing material, with attribution, on a perpetual, non-exclusive,
    royalty-free basis — since such content is already public.
5.3 Company's right to produce NEW promotional material using Distributor's
    name, likeness, or clips of not-yet-published content requires the
    relationship to be active. This right lapses upon termination of
    this Agreement, unless renewed by mutual written agreement.
5.4 Company will provide Distributor any product screenshots, demo access,
    or explainer material reasonably needed to produce accurate
    content.

6. NO EXCLUSIVITY
This Agreement does not grant Distributor exclusivity in any city,
category, or vertical unless separately agreed in writing.

7. REPRESENTATIONS
7.1 Distributor will not make false, misleading, or exaggerated claims
    about FormYaar's service, pricing, government affiliation, or data
    handling in their content.
7.2 Company represents that FormYaar does not store full Aadhaar
    numbers (only last 4 digits, as permitted) and does not
    misrepresent data handling to end users.

8. TERM & TERMINATION
8.1 This Agreement is fixed at 15 days from the Effective Date and
    does not auto-renew. Upon expiry, the parties may choose to sign a
    new agreement (a further pilot term, or a longer/monthly
    arrangement) based on the outcome of this period.
8.2 Either party may end this Agreement before the 15 days are up by
    written notice (email is sufficient), for any reason.
8.3 Termination or expiry immediately deactivates the Coupon Code per
    4.2, but does not affect commission already earned on Tracked
    Referrals recorded prior to deactivation — such amounts remain
    payable per the payment schedule in Section 2.2.

9. LIABILITY
9.1 Company will provide refunds to end customers for failed
    autofill/application attempts per its standard refund policy.
9.2 Company is not liable for any indirect loss to Distributor's channel,
    reputation, or audience relationship arising from technical
    issues, beyond the refund remedy to the affected end customer.
